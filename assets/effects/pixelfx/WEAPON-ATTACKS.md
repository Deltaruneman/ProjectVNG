Additional melee sheets exported from the bundled Pixelfx library at `assets/vendor/pixelfx-main/pixelfx-main/` using its `tools/render.mjs --sheet` workflow. Source definitions and full MIT license remain in that library. Sheets are unchanged exports and use the library's embedded frame duration, pivot and palette metadata. Project runtime applies nearest-neighbour filtering and tints each main-weapon effect by its fixed element.

Exports: `melee/sword-slash`, `melee/thrust`, `melee/dagger-flurry`, and `impacts/ground-slam`. Existing `melee/spear-sweep` and `melee/claw-swipe` are reused.
